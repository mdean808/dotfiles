## voice-and-tone: Everyday tasks and activities

Source: Shopify/polaris@d7dc443:polaris.shopify.com/content/content/voice-and-tone.md

- Do: Look for the simplest way to communicate information—words aren’t always necessary.
- Don't: Use overly complicated or intimidating language.

## voice-and-tone: Don’t

Source: Shopify/polaris@d7dc443:polaris.shopify.com/content/content/voice-and-tone.md

- Do: Be consistent for identical actions or destinations when possible.
- Do: _Delete product_
- Do: _Delete collection_
- Don't: Add extra text just to fill space.

## voice-and-tone: Learning and education

Source: Shopify/polaris@d7dc443:polaris.shopify.com/content/content/voice-and-tone.md

- Do: Help people understand why they should do something, not just how.
- Don't: Oversell or overpromise.
- Don't: _Create a new campaign and you could double your sales this holiday season._

## voice-and-tone: Don’t

Source: Shopify/polaris@d7dc443:polaris.shopify.com/content/content/voice-and-tone.md

- Do: Break down complicated tasks into steps that help people focus on one thing at a time.
- Don't: Be overly prescriptive about how someone should use a feature.
- Don't: _You need to add at least 10 products before opening your store._

## voice-and-tone: Simple errors

Source: Shopify/polaris@d7dc443:polaris.shopify.com/content/content/voice-and-tone.md

- Do: Clearly explain the situation and how it can be resolved.
- Do: _Product weight needs to be positive. Change the product weight to be greater than or equal to 0 and try again._
- Don't: Use overly dramatic or scary words for simple errors.
- Don't: _Bad request, forbidden, fatal, expectation failed, unresolved, invalid_

## voice-and-tone: Don’t

Source: Shopify/polaris@d7dc443:polaris.shopify.com/content/content/voice-and-tone.md

- Do: Look for ways to help people resolve problems automatically.
- Don't: Use error numbers or code unless they’re easily recognizable by people and help them solve the problem.

## voice-and-tone: Acknowledging effort, progress, or completion

Source: Shopify/polaris@d7dc443:polaris.shopify.com/content/content/voice-and-tone.md

- Do: If this is a task people do regularly, make it clear that the step is complete in a simple and non-intrusive way, like a [toast](/components/toast). Consider ways to confirm completion without words or messaging.
- Do: _Product saved_
- Don't: Assume people are excited or celebrating. They may be annoyed if it was a particularly long process or if the immediate benefits aren’t clear.
- Don't: _Congrats! You set up your single login for Shopify._

## voice-and-tone: Don’t

Source: Shopify/polaris@d7dc443:polaris.shopify.com/content/content/voice-and-tone.md

- Do: If the task was something we initiated or required, thank them for their time.
- Do: _Thanks for taking the time to share your feedback._
- Don't: Refer to simple actions or completed steps as “successes.”
- Don't: _You successfully added a product._

## voice-and-tone: Motivate or encourage action

Source: Shopify/polaris@d7dc443:polaris.shopify.com/content/content/voice-and-tone.md

- Do: Help people understand what the next steps are and why they should take them.
- Do: _Your email address is connected to 8 accounts. Set up a single login to switch between stores faster and log in less often._
- Don't: Assume the next step or outcome is guaranteed.
- Don't: _You’re just a few steps away from receiving your first order._

## voice-and-tone: Don’t

Source: Shopify/polaris@d7dc443:polaris.shopify.com/content/content/voice-and-tone.md

- Do: Provide guidance to help people understand if the decision is right for them.
- Do: _Based on your type of business, this is the POS hardware we recommend:._

## voice-and-tone: Serious problems or errors

Source: Shopify/polaris@d7dc443:polaris.shopify.com/content/content/voice-and-tone.md

- Do: Explain the impact on their business clearly, without using confusing or scary language.
- Do: _Some of today’s sales data hasn’t been updated yet. This will be fixed shortly. Your data is safe, and your sales are not affected._
- Don't: Ask merchants to trust us without providing data or context.
- Don't: _Today’s sales data might not be accurate, but don’t worry—it’s just temporary._

## voice-and-tone: Don’t

Source: Shopify/polaris@d7dc443:polaris.shopify.com/content/content/voice-and-tone.md

- Do: Apologize and empathize when we’re at fault, but within reason.
- Do: _All systems are now fully operational. We recognize and apologize for the stress, concern, and impact this outage had on your business. In the coming days we will be working to fully understand how this widespread internet infrastructure failure affected our platform._

## voice-and-tone: Do

Source: Shopify/polaris@d7dc443:polaris.shopify.com/content/content/voice-and-tone.md

- Do: Provide clear steps to resolve issues. If there’s no solution, be direct about what that means for the merchant and what we’re doing about it.
- Do: _Your Shopify Payments account is on hold. Please attach your State Tax Exemption Certificate for review._

## voice-and-tone: Announcing new features and updates

Source: Shopify/polaris@d7dc443:polaris.shopify.com/content/content/voice-and-tone.md

- Do: _**Introducing the new Buy Button**_
- Do: _Make it your own with custom fonts, colors, and styles. It also loads 50% faster so your visitors won’t miss it._
- Don't: Just talk about ourselves and assume the experience for merchants will be easy.
- Don't: _**Get Shopify’s new Buy Button**_

## voice-and-tone: Don’t

Source: Shopify/polaris@d7dc443:polaris.shopify.com/content/content/voice-and-tone.md

## voice-and-tone: Celebrations and congratulations

Source: Shopify/polaris@d7dc443:polaris.shopify.com/content/content/voice-and-tone.md

- Do: Only celebrate truly important or monumental situations when the audience likely feels proud, excited, or satisfied.
- Do: _Your store is 1 year old today! Here’s to many more._
- Don't: Celebrate completing tasks or processes that people don’t actually want to celebrate. Did they finish something difficult or do something for us? You may want to acknowledge their effort instead.
- Don't: _You set up your payment providers, congrats!_

Source: Shopify/polaris@d7dc443:polaris.shopify.com/content/content/voice-and-tone.md

- Don't: Take credit for merchants’ successes or presume to know what they need.
- Don't: _We did it! Congrats on your first sale._

## error-messages: Avoid using toast for error messages

Source: Shopify/polaris@d7dc443:polaris.shopify.com/content/patterns/error-messages.md

- Don't: ### Don’t use modals for errors
- Don't: Modal dialogs are a good way to ask merchants to confirm a destructive action, but not to tell them an error has occurred. Modals block merchants until a decision is made, which is likely to make merchants feel pressured. Most errors don’t need to block access to the rest of the feature.
- Don't: ### Avoid using [home notifications](#home-notifications) for errors
- Don't: Home notification errors are for high-priority tasks that merchants must complete immediately to continue using Shopify or prevent a negative impact to their business, like losing money. One exception is errors for features that don‘t have a dedicated details page.
